## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **multi-disciplinary engineer** working at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, Software Development and Cybersecurity**. My goal is to design intelligent systems that are **secure by design**.

- 🛡️ **Cybersecurity & Digital Forensics:** malware analysis, kernel-level exploitation research, and detecting AI-generated deepfakes and metadata forgery.
- ⚙️ **Reverse Engineering:** static and dynamic binary analysis with **IDA Pro** and **Ghidra**.
- 🤖 **AI & Machine Learning:** computer-vision models (CNNs, ViTs, spectrogram analysis) for media integrity and classification.
- 📱 **Software Engineering:** secure, performant cross-platform apps with **Flutter** and **Dart**, backed by **FastAPI / Flask / Firebase**.
- 🎓 **Education:** B.Sc. in Computer Science & Information, **Benha University** — specialization in **Information Security & Digital Forensics** (Very Good). Graduation project *Forgery Detection*: **Excellent**.
- 🏅 **Certificate:** *Cross-Platform Mobile App Development using Flutter* — 102 hours, ITI (Ministry of Communications), Jul–Aug 2026.
- 💼 **Freelance:** I build apps for clients (e.g. a football matches app, Solara).
- 🎤 **Community:** I give talks to students on the risks and benefits of AI and how to use it responsibly.
- 🎯 **Now:** polishing my forensics project and preparing for cybersecurity certifications.
